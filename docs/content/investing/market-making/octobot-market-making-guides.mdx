# OctoBot Market Making Guides

## OctoBot Market Making, your self-service market making platform

[OctoBot Market Making](https://market-making.octobot.cloud) makes it simple to add liquidity to a centralized exchange crypto market.

<YouTube id="8ayhxShhaW0" title="OctoBot Market Making - an Alternative Market Making Platform" />

It is designed to help crypto projects, exchanges and individual market makers to use advanced automated market making strategies using a transparent self-service platform.

## Getting started with OctoBot Market Making

As a self-service platform, once your account is active, you will be able to start, configure and stop your automated market making strategies by yourself.

Just [contact the team](mailto:contact@octobot.cloud) or [book a call](https://cal.com/octobot.cloud/market-making-30min) to get your account ready and [start your market making bot](starting-your-market-making-bot).

## Configuring and protecting your funds

OctoBot Market Making provides [funds management and automated protection systems](configuring-and-protecting-your-market-making-funds) to safeguard your budget.

This includes:

- Maximum spending limits
- Pump & dump protection
- Emergency stops for market crashes
- And more ...

## Using a fair price for your market making strategy

OctoBot Market Making lets you configure your market making strategy over many different parameters, including the price that will be used to create your order book from.

![octobot market making starting bot with formula reference price](/images/guides/octobot-market-making-starting-bot-with-formula-reference-price.png)

You can configure your reference price using:

- The price of your traded market on your exchange
- The price of your traded market from other exchange(s)
- A static price
- A formula to dynamically compute the price from an average value, or enforce support levels or resistance levels.

More details about the different ways to configure a market making reference price can be found in the [reference price formulas guide](using-formulas-to-configure-your-market-making-reference-price).

## Supported exchanges

OctoBot Market Making supports **most centralized exchanges**. In case you would like to see an exchange added to the list, please [contact the team](mailto:contact@octobot.cloud).

View all supported exchanges on the [exchanges list](https://market-making.octobot.cloud/supported-exchanges).

## Market making from a browser and a mobile app

OctoBot Market Making is available from a browser and from a mobile app, in order to make it as convenient as possible to monitor and adapt your market making strategy:

- According to your needs
- Whenever necessary
- In any situation, even if you are on the go with limited internet access

## Understanding the Liquidity Score

The Liquidity Score is a number between 0 and 10 that rates a market's liquidity based on market standards.

![octobot market making liquidity scores preview](/images/guides/octobot-market-making-liquidity-scores-preview.jpg)

The higher the score, the more liquid the market. Learn more on the [Liquidity Score guide](understanding-the-liquidity-score).
